Facility rules

FieldhouseUSA is a private, commercial facility, not a recreation center. We follow health code and our own policies. By entering you accept these rules and the waiver terms. That covers players and spectators. You enter at your own risk.

No outside food or drink. Everyone is subject to a bag search. Fines start at the first offense.

Sign the waiverRules as a PDF
Before you enter
  • We may refuse entry or ask anyone to leave for any reason.
  • Everyone who enters is subject to a bag search.
  • Photos and video taken in the facility belong to the facility and may be used in marketing. Security cameras and staff monitor the building.
  • For tournaments and events run by outside organizers, FieldhouseUSA provides the venue only. We are not responsible for how the event is organized, or for injuries, damages or losses during it. Contact your organizer with event questions.
Bags and weapons
  • No weapons or firearms of any kind.
  • Only player backpacks are allowed inside. Purses and camera bags are permitted but must be open for a check.
  • All bags are subject to search at the door and inside the building.
Food and drink
  • No outside commercial food or drink, including coffee. No coolers of food.
  • No gum. No sports drinks, coffee or tea in playing areas. They cause slips and delays.
  • Only sealed, clear water is allowed on the courts. No Stanley cups or uncapped cups.
  • No eating or snacks on the courts. A bar and kitchen is on site. All alcohol stays in the bar seating area.
  • If you bring outside food, eat it inside your vehicle.
Courts and equipment
  • Don't climb nets, fences or sprinklers, stand on tables or bar tops, or enter non-play areas. Offenders pay for damages.
  • No personal chairs, camping chairs, coolers or self-carried team tables on the courts. Don't move court chairs.
  • Floor mats and seats are fine if they don't scratch the floor. Movable spectator chairs are first come, first served. Please return them.
  • No ball handling or play, including dribbling and throwing, outside authorized event areas.
  • Keep team belongings in your team area and out of the play space, so the court stays clear for serving and defense.
  • Clean spills immediately. The responsible team stops play to clean up. Each team must clean its area after playing or be fined, even if the trash isn't yours.
Kids and restricted areas
  • Children must be supervised at all times or will be asked to leave.
  • Stay out of areas you are not booked in: the studios, the lounge, the therapy room, training bays and anything closed or under construction.
Animals and service animals
  • No animals, except service animals as defined by law.
  • Bringing a service animal? Tell us before you arrive. We need time to arrange a reasonable accommodation for you and your animal.
  • Animals are not allowed on playing surfaces or in activity areas. That is for the safety of the animal and the players.
First aid and power
  • There is no athletic trainer and no ice on site. Bring your own. First-aid kits and bandages can be purchased for a fee.
  • No appliances may be plugged in. Phone charging is allowed around the bar areas only.
Parking and out front
  • Parking is free and plentiful, but we are not responsible for your vehicle. Lock it and keep valuables out of sight.
  • No tables, camping or tailgating in front of the building. Aurora has a camping ban and other businesses operate here. Unattended items are removed.
  • No littering in the lot.
Lost and found
  • There is no lost and found. Unclaimed items are disposed of nightly for sanitation. Lost electronics and valuables are not our responsibility. Please don't call or email about lost items.
USAV RMR events
  • Every RMR team coach signs the facility agreement and is responsible for their players and families following these rules.
  • RMR players only may bring a brown-bag lunch carried in a player backpack. If it doesn't fit in the backpack, it isn't allowed. Spectators may not bring bags, except purses and camera bags, or food.
  • Doors open at 7:00 AM. Team areas, tables and chairs are first come, first served. The coaches' meeting is at 7:30 AM at the admin desk.
  • Warm-up balls and carts are not provided. RMR provides one game ball per court. Guest Wi-Fi is available.
  • A limited number of court-approved folding chairs are provided free. Return them to the rack at the end. If chairs aren't returned, the privilege ends and chairs become paid rentals.
  • Coaches and directors must check out with the site director before leaving, or final scores won't be entered. That is an automatic forfeit.
Spectators and fines
  • Violations are monitored on camera.
  • Spectators who refuse to comply will be asked to leave or escorted out by Aurora police. Spectators must supervise their own children.
  • These rules are the only warning. Fines start at the first offense.

Questions

Is FieldhouseUSA a city rec center?

No. It is a private, commercial sports and event facility. Pricing and rules are not the same as a school or rec center.

Can I bring outside food or drink?

No. Outside food and drink, including coffee, are not allowed in the building. Oda Up serves food and drinks inside. Event catering goes through Event-Den.

When is drop-in volleyball?

Thursday through Monday, 7 PM to 11 PM. Boomtown Athletics runs it. See the drop-in page.

How far ahead do tournaments book?

Six to eight months. All rentals require a signed rental contract. A 30% deposit holds the dates and the balance is due at least 30 days before the event. Tournament details.

What are your hours?

Hours follow the day's programs and events. Check your program's schedule or your event confirmation before you come.

Is there parking?

Yes, free and on site. It is an active lot, so lock your car and keep valuables out of sight.

Are service animals allowed?

Yes. Tell us before you arrive so we have time to arrange a reasonable accommodation. Animals are not allowed on playing surfaces.
