    Questions

    Good to know.

    How long is a party?

    The party room is booked in 3-hour blocks, and setup and cleanup are part of the 3 hours. Courts, Zone 7, the lounge and Shoot 360 are booked by the hour.

    How does payment work?

    Payment is up front, and a security deposit covers cleaning and damage. If your date is more than 3 months away, a 30% deposit holds it and the rest is due 1 to 2 months before the party. Tax is added to food and drinks.

    Can we bring our own food?

    Booked parties can, for a $250 outside food fee when you skip the Oda Up food packages. Outside alcohol is not allowed. Everyone else follows the building rule: no outside food or drink.

    Do guests sign a waiver?

    Yes. Everyone who plays on a court or an inflatable signs the liability waiver first. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.

    Is there a deal for Colorado Boom members?

    Yes. Club members get one volleyball court for the 3 hours, included with the party room.

    What if my event is bigger?

    Events over 75 guests, events longer than a day, and events that need staging, sound, décor or vendors are planned by Event-Den, the event company in the building. It hosts up to 600 guests.

    Do the facility rules apply?

    Yes, to every guest. Read the facility rules before party day.

    Planning something bigger?

    Corporate events, cultural events, expos, conventions and galas, up to 600 guests. Event-Den plans and runs them, with Oda Up on food and bar.
