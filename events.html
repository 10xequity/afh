    Questions

    Good to know.

    How long is a party?

    Three hours, with setup and cleanup inside the 3 hours. The party room, courts, corral areas and Zone 7 are all booked for the same 3-hour block. The Social-Den lounge and Shoot 360 are booked by the hour.

    When can we book a party?

    In winter, most openings are Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights, with some Monday and Tuesday nights from 7:30 PM. Summer has more open dates, depending on the events in the building.

    How many guests fit?

    A volleyball court suits 12 to 20 players and a corral area holds 20 to 30 people. Parties here go up to 75 guests. Bigger groups are planned by Event-Den.

    How does payment work?

    Payment is up front, and a $300 security deposit covers cleaning and damage. If your date is more than 3 months away, a 30% deposit holds it and the rest is due 1 to 2 months before the party. Tax is added to food and drinks.

    What if we cancel?

    If you cancel within 60 days of your party, the 30% deposit is not refunded.

    Can we bring our own food?

    Booked parties can, for a $250 outside food fee when you skip the Oda Up food packages. Outside alcohol is not allowed. Everyone else follows the building rule: no outside food or drink.

    Do guests sign a waiver?

    Yes. Everyone who plays on a court or an inflatable signs the liability waiver first. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.

    Is there a deal for Colorado Boom members?

    Yes. Club members get one volleyball court for the 3 hours, included with the party room.

    What if my event is bigger?

    Events over 75 guests, events longer than a day, and events that need staging, sound, décor or vendors are planned by Event-Den, the event company in the building. It hosts up to 600 guests, or up to 1,000 across the whole building with advance planning.

    Do the facility rules apply?

    Yes, to every guest. Read the facility rules before party day.

    Planning something bigger?

    Corporate events, cultural events, expos, conventions and galas, up to 600 guests, or up to 1,000 across the whole building with advance planning. Event-Den plans and runs them, with Oda Up on food and bar.
