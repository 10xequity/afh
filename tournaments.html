Tournaments and leagues

Host your tournament here.

FieldhouseUSA rents courts and floor space to tournament directors, leagues and sanctioning bodies. You run the event. We provide the building: 15 volleyball courts in seven zones, 32,000 sq ft of open floor, a bar and kitchen, seating and free parking, in Aurora on the east side of the Denver metro.

What we host

Fifteen courts, 32,000 sq ft of open floor and rooms for everything around the competition. Run a single-day tournament or a multi-day championship: court sports on the hardwood and sports courts, then raise the nets for mat sports, meets, showcases, expos and ceremonies. Camps, clinics, combines, league finals, festivals and banquets all fit under the same roof.

Volleyball

Up to 15 courts. Sanctioned and independent events for juniors and adults.

  • Region and national qualifier play dates
  • Club, school and adult tournaments and leagues
  • Pods of 2, 3 or 4 courts, or the whole building

Basketball and pickleball

Zones 1 to 4 convert from volleyball.

  • 4 basketball courts
  • 12 pickleball courts
  • Club tournaments, showcases and league finals

Futsal

Zone 7 sets up as one futsal arena.

  • Single-arena tournaments and festivals
  • Can run beside a court-sport event

Martial arts

Rings laid out across the open floor, with room for spectators around them.

  • Karate, taekwondo, judo and jiu-jitsu tournaments
  • Belt testing, seminars and camps
  • Vendor and school tables beside the rings

Wrestling and mat sports

Mats go down on the open floor with the nets raised.

  • Wrestling and grappling tournaments and duals
  • Several mats running at once
  • Camps and clinics

Gymnastics, cheer and dance

Open floor for competition areas, warm-up space and seating.

  • Gymnastics meets and showcases
  • Cheer and dance competitions
  • Two studios for warm-ups and judging rooms

Open space and rooms

Every net raises to the ceiling. That clears the floor for mat events, expos, galas, ceremonies and anything that needs room, with space for school and vendor tables beside the action.

Expos, conferences and exhibitions

Room for the booths, the stage and the crowd.

With the nets raised, the floor becomes an exhibit hall. Lay out vendor rows on the hardwood, put a main stage at one end, run breakouts in the studios and host the reception on the mezzanine. Oda Up feeds everyone without leaving the building.

  • Trade shows, sports and fitness expos, and vendor fairs
  • Conferences, summits and award banquets
  • Exhibitions, showcases and product launches
  • Combines, recruiting days and registration nights

Game day

Built for play

Hardwood and sports-court floors, nets at every height, and the whole day under one roof: warm-ups, matches, food, finals and awards.

Room for the crowd

Bleachers along the courts, the mezzanine above them and Oda Up courtside, so families and fans stay for the whole day.

Cultural and community events

Bring your whole community

Community associations and cultural groups host their tournaments here: flags on the wall, families in the stands, matches all day and trophies at the end. Courts, seating and food from Oda Up are all in the building.

How booking works

  1. Ask 6 to 8 months outWeekends fill early. Send your dates, sport, court count and expected teams.
  2. Contract and 30% depositRental contracts are required for all rentals. A signed contract and a 30% deposit hold your dates. Nothing is held without both.
  3. Paid in full 30 days beforeThe balance is due at least 30 days before the first day of the event.
  4. Event dayYour director checks in at the admin desk. Your teams and spectators follow the facility rules.

What to know before you ask

You run the event

  • The organizer handles registration, schedules, officials, scoring and results
  • The organizer is responsible for teams, coaches and spectators following the rules
  • FieldhouseUSA provides the venue only

House rules apply

  • No outside food or drink. Food and the bar come from Oda Up
  • Everyone is subject to a bag search
  • Fines start at the first offense and are charged to the team or club
Read the rules

This is a commercial facility

  • Pricing is not in line with schools or rec centers
  • Single-court, few-hour requests are limited on event days
  • Private parties and corporate events go through Event-Den
